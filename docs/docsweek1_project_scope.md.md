

| Project Name | ResponsibleAI \- Responsible AI Guideline Navigator |
| :---- | :---- |
| Domain | AI Governance & Ethics / Responsible AI (Malaysia and ASEAN context) |
| Problem Statement  | Guidance on responsible AI is exposed across several long official documentations (national,regional and international) where its written in a formal policy language. New students and junior practitioners often find it difficult to locate and interpret relevant principles quickly. AI engineering makes this possible to build a document-grounded question-answering assistant that retrieves the relevant passages and answers with appropriate citations. |
| Evidence of the Problem  | Evidence 1: Malaysia's AI governance ecosystem involves multiple stakeholders and governance initiatives. The ISIS Malaysia white paper recommends stronger coordination of AI policymaking and governance, highlighting the need for better coordination across the ecosystem. Source / citation: Institute of Strategic and International Studies (ISIS) Malaysia. *AI Governance White Paper.* [https\://www\.mydigital.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/AI-Governance\_White\_Paper-ISIS.pd](https://www.mydigital.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/AI-Governance_White_Paper-ISIS.pdf)f  Evidence 2: Responsible AI guidance covers multiple principles, including fairness, transparency, safety, privacy, accountability and human oversight. These principles are documented across international and regional frameworks, requiring users to consult relevant sections to answer specific questions. Source / citation: ASEAN Secretariat. *ASEAN Guide on AI Governance and Ethics* (2024). [https\://asean.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/ASEAN-Guide-on-AI-Governance-and-Ethics\_beautified\_201223\_v2.pdf](https://asean.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/ASEAN-Guide-on-AI-Governance-and-Ethics_beautified_201223_v2.pdf)  |

Study / solution 2: General-purpose AI assistants (e.g. ChatGPT, Claude)  Source / citation: [https\://chatgpt.com/](https://chatgpt.com/)  Key limitation: Flexible, but answers may not be grounded in the official text and may hallucinate  Study / solution 3: NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)  Source / citation:  Tabassi, E. | Engineering Gap / Limitation  | No citation-grounded assistant with safety guardrails exists for navigating Malaysian and ASEAN responsible AI guidelines, so users must manually search fragmented documents or rely on ungrounded general-purpose chatbots. ResponsibleAI will address this practical gap by developing a retrieval-augmented question-answering assistant focused on Malaysian, ASEAN and international responsible AI guidelines, with source-grounded answers, citation verification and safety guardrails.  |
| Initial Project Objectives (2–3)  | O1: To design a document-grounded assistant for navigating responsible AI guidelines, based on official public documents.  O2: To develop the assistant using retrieval-augmented generation with guardrails (scope check, citation check, "not legal advice" handling)  O3: To evaluate the assistant in terms of answer accuracy, groundedness, retrieval quality, response latency and safety.  |
| Target Users  | AI students and junior AI practitioners who need quick, cited answers about responsible AI guidelines  |
